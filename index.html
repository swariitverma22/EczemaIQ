DERMATOLOGY GUIDANCE LIBRARY

Guidance behind the plan.

EczemaIQ uses a transparent rule library based on dermatology guidance for common skin-care, trigger, and swimming precautions. It complements—not replaces—your dermatologist’s treatment plan.

  • 01

    Moisturize after bathing. Applying a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer while skin is still damp helps support hydration.American Academy of Dermatology ↗

  • 02

    Use gentle bathing and product practices. Lukewarm water plus fragrance-free products and detergents can help protect the skin barrier.National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases ↗

  • 03

    Manage sweat, temperature, and fabrics. Heat, sweat, wool, and synthetic fabrics can be common triggers for some people.American Academy of Dermatology ↗

  • 04

    Use a swim routine when pool water irritates. Dermatologists recommend a barrier moisturizer before pool time, then rinsing and moisturizing promptly afterwards.American Academy of Dermatology ↗

  • 05

    Discuss possible food allergies with a clinician. EczemaIQ does not diagnose food triggers or recommend food elimination on its own.National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases ↗

The score, thresholds, and suggested timing in this prototype are decision-support heuristics; they are not a validated medical prediction or medication guidance.
